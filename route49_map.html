Getting to Route 49

Route 49 sits past Mt. Silver, behind a Waterfall climb. Traced from this repo's own map connection data (map.json warps and connections) — not guessed from memory.

  1. Start at Route 28

    The last stretch of road before the mountain. Head toward Mt. Silver's entrance.

  2. Mt. Silver — Outside

    Walk in; this links straight back to Route 28, so it's the only door in.

  3. Mt. Silver 1F, Waterfall Room HM Waterfall

    A scientist NPC marks this room; the climb itself needs the Waterfall HM on a Pokémon in your party. A warp here leads into Snowswept Cavern.

  4. Snowswept Cavern

    A short connecting cave — no detours, it opens straight onto Route 49.

  5. Route 49

    You've arrived. Wild Kangaskhan shows up here too.

Past Route 49: heading further north chains into New Sinjoh → Route 50 → Sinjoh Ruins, the legendary-quest cluster (Regirock/Regice/Registeel/Regieleki/Regidrago/Regigigas/Arceus rooms). None of that area connects back out anywhere else in the map data — Mt. Silver's Waterfall Room is the only door in or out.
